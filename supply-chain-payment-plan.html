Supply Chain Payment Procedure and Action Plan

Adopted 9 November 2026 · Onarvo Ltd (Company No. 17040899)

📄 Download Full Plan (PDF)

Purpose

This document sets out how Onarvo Ltd will pay its supply chain promptly and report payment performance.

Current Position

Onarvo Ltd was incorporated on 18 February 2026. It does not yet have two complete six-month reporting periods of supply-chain payment data. No payment performance figures are claimed.

Standard Payment Terms

Onarvo Ltd will:

  • Pay undisputed supplier invoices within 30 days of receipt, or sooner where agreed
  • Include payment terms of no more than 30 days in all supply-chain contracts for public contracts
  • Require the same terms to be passed down the supply chain (Procurement Act 2023, sections 68 and 73)

Disputed Invoices

Onarvo Ltd will:

  • Notify the supplier of any dispute within 5 working days of receipt
  • Pay any undisputed part on time
  • Aim to resolve the dispute within 15 working days, escalating to the Director if unresolved

Late Payment Interest

Where interest is due under contract or the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, it will be paid.

Action Plan

Cause

No complete reporting period yet exists, so 95% of invoices paid within 60 days cannot yet be demonstrated.

Actions:

  • Record invoice receipt and payment dates for every supplier invoice from contract start
  • Report monthly to the Director the % of invoices paid within 30 days, 31-60 days, over 60 days and due but unpaid, and the average days to pay
  • Investigate and correct any invoice paid outside agreed terms

Reporting: A six-monthly summary to the Director, as the company's equivalent of an audit committee.

Publication: This plan is published on the company website.

Sign-off
